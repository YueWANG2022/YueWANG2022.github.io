About

I am a PhD researcher at King's College London and a member of the STaR-AI (Safe, Trusted & Responsible AI) Doctoral Programme. My research focuses on analysing unstructured event data to gain insights into urban dynamics and cultural life, combining large language models (LLMs), geospatial data science and machine learning.

Research Interests

  • Large language models (applications and evaluation)
  • Information extraction and event data analysis
  • Spatial data science and GeoAI
  • Machine learning and applied statistics
